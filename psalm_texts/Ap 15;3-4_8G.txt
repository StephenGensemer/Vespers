Mighty and wónderful are your wórks, * Lord Gód Almíghty!
Righteous and trúe are your wáys, * O Kíng of the nátions!\vspace{15pt}
Who would dare refúse you hónor, * or the glory due your náme, O Lórd?\vspace{15pt}
Since you alóne are hóly, * all nátions shall cóme
and worship in yóur présence. * Your mighty déeds are clearly séen.\vspace{15pt}
Glory be to the Father, and tó the Són, * and to the Hóly Ghóst; 
As it was in the beginning, † is now, and éver sháll be, * world without énd. Amén.
