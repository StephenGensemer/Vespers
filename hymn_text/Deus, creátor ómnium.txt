Deus, creátor ómnium 
políque rector, véstiens 
diem decóro lúmine, 
noctem sopóris grátia,

Artus solútos ut quies 
reddat labóris úsui 
mentésque fessas állevet 
luctúsque solvat ánxios,

Grates perácto iam die 
et noctis exórtu preces, 
voti reos ut ádiuves, 
hymnum canéntes sólvimus.

Te cordis ima cóncinant, 
te vox canóra cóncrepet, 
te díligat castus amor, 
te mens adóret sóbria,

Ut cum profúnda cláuserit 
diem calígo nóctium, 
fides ténebras nésciat 
et nox fide relúceat.

Christum rogámus et Patrem, 
Christi Patrísque Spíritum; 
unum potens per ómnia, 
fove precántes, Trínitas. Amen.

O God, Creator of all things
And Ruler of heaven, clothing
The day with comely light
And the night with the grace of sleep,

So that rest may make our limbs
Return to the employment of work
And alleviate our tired souls
And relieve our troubled grief,

Grateful for the day now drawing to a close and
At the beginning of nightfall, entoning a hymn of petition,
We pour forth [our] prayers so that
You may help those burdened with guilt.

May the depths of our heart praise you in song,
May our voice resound in cries to you,
May our chaste ardour love you,
May our sober soul adore you,

So that, when the deep darkness of nights
Brings to a close the day,
Our faith may know no shadows
And the night shine again with faith.

We beseech Christ and the Father,
And the Spirit of Christ and of the Father,
Mighty One in all things, O Trinity:
Cherish those who invoke you. Amen.
